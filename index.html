Why

  1. It costs you ten seconds and me twenty minutes. Generating is free. Reading isn't. You just moved the work to me.
  2. Length is not thinking. Six sections and a risk matrix tell me you pressed a button, not that you have something to say.
  3. I can't tell what's yours. Which claims did you check? Which ones do you believe? If you don't know, I definitely don't.

The rules

01
Send the conclusion. Three sentences. If you can't write them, you're not done thinking.
02
Send the prompt, not the output. The question you asked is usually more useful than the answer you got.
03
If you must send it, point. "Section 3 is the part I agree with. Ignore the rest." Now it's useful.
04
Read it before I do. All of it. If it bored you, it'll bore me.
